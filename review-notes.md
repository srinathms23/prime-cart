# Visual Verification Notes

## Desktop Review

The 1280px storefront preserves the intended Sunlit Mercantile direction: a warm porcelain palette, ink-navy promotional anchor, persimmon offer language, editorial serif hierarchy, and catalogue-style hero collage. The review prompted a final enhancement pass that introduced consistent paper-framed product media, distinctive cut-paper offer tickets, a restrained mercantile category shelf, and an editorial interlude in the product collection.

## Mobile Review

At 390px wide, navigation utilities compress appropriately into a menu and compact cart/account controls. The category rail remains horizontally browseable, the hero and promotional cards stack without overlap, product information remains readable, and the full shopping flow retains clear call-to-action targets. No visible overflow or contrast issue was observed in the capture.

## Build Verification

TypeScript type checking and production build both completed successfully after the final visual refinement pass. The only build output is the existing advisory about bundle size, which does not block the static storefront preview.
