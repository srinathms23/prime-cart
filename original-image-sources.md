## Review scope

The register deliberately identifies **product pages** separately from **product-family or collection pages**. A collection reference supports editorial replacement and provenance review but is not evidence that the staged image is licensed for commercial reuse. No customer review, rating, discount, or merchant-availability claim is inferred from these pages.
